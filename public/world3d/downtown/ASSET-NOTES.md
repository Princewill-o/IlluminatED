# Downtown City MegaKit (Standard, free) by Quaternius

- Source: https://quaternius.itch.io/downtown-city-megakit (downloaded from itch.io, November 2026)
- Licence: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain), see LICENSE.txt. Credit is optional; we credit Quaternius anyway.
- Converted from the glTF export to GLB: base-colour textures only (normal and ORM maps dropped,
  the game shades and outlines everything itself), textures resized to 512 px WebP.
- Used for: brick apartment blocks in the city centre (`building-small`, `building-medium`) and
  street planters beside them. `bollard` and `ac-unit` are spare props.
