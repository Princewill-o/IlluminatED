# Creative Characters (free pack)

- Source: "Creative Characters Free" (.blend and GLB), supplied by the project owner in November 2026.
- `creative-kit.glb` is made from the pack's combined GLB: costume pieces, the clown nose, the pacifier and
  one glove set removed; only everyday clips kept (idle, look, relaxed, walk, walk-back, run, jump, jump-loop,
  land, fall, climb, crouch, strafe). Fight, hit, block and death clips are not shipped.
- `thumbs/` are rendered from the kit in the game (dev page `/dev/characters?mode=thumbs`) with a drawn outline.
