# Emergency vehicles

- `ambulance.glb`: original IlluminatED paramedic van generated by `scripts/world/build_emergency_vehicles.py`. Generic green/yellow markings and medical plus; no protected Red Cross emblem. Hollow patient compartment, stretcher and separate RearDoorLeft/RearDoorRight hinge nodes.
- `police.glb`: adapted from the supplied Quaternius Realistic Car Pack Cop.glb, CC0 1.0. Original opaque body/wheels retained, source tiny roof-lamp faces removed, added blue/yellow panels, POLICE geometry lettering and larger blue lightbar.
- Quaternius source notice is in ../cars-realistic/LICENSE.txt.

Runtime API: `createEmergencyVehicle(kind, load, style)` from `src/components/game/world/emergency-vehicle3d.ts`. Returned root is centered on X/Z, grounded at Y0 and faces +X; rear is -X. Ambulance closed envelope is at most 4.8 × 1.75 world tiles. Police uses the existing 3.8 × 1.65 car envelope. Uniform fitting preserves wheel and body proportions.

`openRearDoors(progress)` clamps 0–1 and opens the ambulance doors outward towards -X. `update(seconds, sirens)` alternates independently cloned blue lightbar materials. `dispose()` releases only per-instance materials; cached shared model geometry remains reusable. App controls route, transform, rescue sequence and audio.

`realisticCarAsset('ambulance'|'police')` also resolves to these assets; normal cars retain the supplied pack. Motor-car fallback sprites for ambulance and police have been regenerated from the matching emergency GLBs. Preview images are rendered from the same geometry.
