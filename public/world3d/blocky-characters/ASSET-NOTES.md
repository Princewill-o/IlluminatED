# Blocky Characters

The GLB character models and thumbnails in this folder come from the free sample packs by Popyca / Vuena. Both supplied versions are licensed CC0 1.0. The primary version includes idle, walk, run, jump, fall, wave, punch, death, cheer and dance clips. The classic edition models are also kept as separate selectable designs and include their own embedded textures and animation clips.

The second `Blocky-Characters-FREE-Sample-8-2` folder contained duplicate copies of the primary sample and did not need a separate import.
