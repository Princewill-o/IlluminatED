# Workplace learning materials and practical assessment plan

Research checked 18 October 2026. This is a sourced curriculum plan, not an implemented course or an employer partnership. Sources were located through their publishers' pages/search listings; individual modules, videos, licences and account requirements still need review before embedding or adapting content. Link to sources; do not copy their course content or certificates.

## Current implementation gap

`careers.ts` has eight role ladders: Pizzazz, Google, Microsoft, Barclays, St Plaster's, Tesco, Lawz and Netflix. Other company venues primarily use revision generators. Hotels currently provide exploration/rest, not assessed hospitality training. Existing lessons mostly repeat answer explanations. Some higher-rank tasks replace role-specific work with a generic invoice problem. A collection of links alone will not resolve that gap.

## Teaching flow inside the game

1. Walk to the relevant desk/counter/tool station and receive a concrete work brief.
2. Read a short original lesson before attempting the task. State learning objectives, prerequisite knowledge, source and review date. Link to a publisher video where available; clearly label narrated original slides as slides, not a sourced video.
3. Inspect a realistic original artefact: ticket, spreadsheet, diary, client file, stock list or log.
4. Perform the task with usable in-game controls. Knowledge checks support the task; they do not replace it.
5. Receive feedback against explicit criteria: accuracy, reasoning, communication and safe escalation. Permit retry with new data.
6. Save the output and rubric results as evidence, labelled "simulated workplace practice". Do not call this employment, accredited training or employer-approved experience.

## Progression and review

- Entry: guided single task with worked example and checklist.
- Intermediate: several linked tasks, incomplete information and an exception to handle.
- Senior: prioritise competing requests, review someone else's output and justify a decision with evidence. Avoid making every senior role harder only through larger numbers or generic invoices.
- Completion requires a correct work product and justification, not minutes watched or repeated guessing. Record hints and retries separately from first-attempt mastery.
- Prioritise the eight existing role ladders first, then build dedicated task generators for engineering, construction, coaching, childcare and travel. Hotels/food venues need explicit roles before being marketed as work experience.
- Obtain subject-specialist review of health, law, finance, childcare, food and engineering scenarios before public claims of subject accuracy. Check source jurisdiction, revision date, accessibility and licensing. Do not publish copied questions, paid course content or third-party certificates.
- Each resource record should store publisher, exact URL, format, access conditions, mapped skills/roles, last checked date, next review date and status. Publisher landing pages can be public while modules require sign-in or payment; verify each chosen module separately.

## Suggested first release

Ship one coherent 10–15 minute scenario per existing workplace: briefing, learning material, realistic artefact, practical response, feedback and saved output. This is a proposed duration, not a provider estimate. Present the remaining workplaces as exploration until their dedicated scenarios are built and reviewed. External source research and this plan do not mean the learning has already been integrated or validated.
