# School access and city update

Platform administrators create an approved school licence in Admin after checking the school's paid agreement. Record its agreement reference, expiry and student seat limit, then invite each verified email with its school and teacher/student role. No automated school payment collection is implied by this workflow.

Teachers land at /teacher, create classes and assign question-bank or custom multiple-choice homework. Students join their own school's class, complete assignments and receive the first-completion reward once. School names and age ranges are private account information. Suspended, expired or full licences cannot admit additional students. Existing individual demonstration invitations remain supported.

Database integration checks: scripts/checks/school-access.sql runs isolated synthetic teacher/student scenarios and rolls them back. School membership, cross-school isolation, seat capacity, repeat reward prevention and suspension were verified against the connected project.

City additions include 33 infill buildings, walkable pizza/supermarket shifts, a university district with official event links, account-scoped wanted state and skippable activity scenes. General event dates appear only when retrieved from official structured event data; otherwise students are directed to the official calendar.

Search metadata uses https://www.illumed.co.uk, matching the public redirect. Set GOOGLE_SITE_VERIFICATION to an ownership token if needed, verify the domain in Google Search Console and submit /sitemap.xml. Indexing and ranking are controlled by search engines and are not immediate or guaranteed.

Actual student/teacher invitation emails must be supplied to create the requested two real accounts. Mobile checks use touch emulation; a physical iPhone Safari check is still recommended.
